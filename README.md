## 🚀 Overview & Cinematic Storytelling Architecture

SkillMate bridges the gap between digital disconnection and real-world passion. Designed specifically for Gen Z, college students, and young working professionals in urban India with an award-winning cinematic storytelling experience inspired by Locomotive.ca, Cula.earth, Penumbra, and Apple.

### 🎬 The 7 Narrative Chapters
1. **HOOK**: Cinematic opening with strong editorial typography, authentic lifestyle photography, and an expanding visual reel that scales into viewport view as you scroll.
2. **PROBLEM (The Silent City)**: Sticky visual scene with moody twilight photography contrasting cold solitary screen time with 3 visceral friction points (The Infinite Scroll Loop, The Dating App Trap, The WhatsApp Blackhole).
3. **DISCOVERY (The Hobby Exhibition)**: Smooth horizontal scrolling gallery featuring large-format editorial cards for acoustic jamming, clay pottery, riverfront cycling, 5v5 turf football, book circles, board games, hackathons, and calisthenics.
4. **CONNECTION (The Spark)**: Pinned convergence animation where two peer cards glide from opposite edges of the screen and lock together at center, plus an interactive micro-radius radar slider (1 km to 20 km).
5. **EXPERIENCE (The Journey)**: Sticky 4-stage storyteller (Cula.earth style) that synchronizes with the interactive mobile phone prototype tabs (Circles, Radar, Squad) as you scroll.
6. **COMMUNITY (Voices & Trust)**: Editorial masonry testimonials from campus students, paired with 4 collegiate trust pillars (College ID verification, AI-approved public venues, live location SOS, and women-only circles).
7. **CTA & TEAM (The Invitation)**: Glowing SkillMate emblem, magnetic "Step Into Your Circle" CTA button, FAQ accordion, and 3D tilt creator cards for Team Spark (Class B8).
1. **The Problem**:
   - **Students & 9-to-5 Workers**: Free time, zero plans. Free evenings with no one to share them with.
   - **New to the City**: Moved for college or a job, zero social circle, isolated.
   - **Hobby Enthusiasts**: Disconnected options, chaotic WhatsApp groups, dead Facebook pages, impersonal mass events.
2. **The Market Gap**:
   - *Hobby/Event Apps (Meetup, Playo, Strava)*: Good for large one-off events or single sports, no casual 1-on-1 chemistry or ongoing intimacy.
   - *Dating/Swiping Apps (Tinder, Bumble BFF)*: Awkward cold chats with no shared context, superficial swipe-first culture leading to ghosting, no persistent groups.
   - *SkillMate Differentiator*: Shared activity feed + verified people, persistent circles (rejoin weekly without re-RSVPing), size-flexible (works for 2–3 people).
3. **The Solution**:
   - **Post & Discover Activities**: Unified hobby feed from 6 AM cycling runs to midnight coding sprints.
   - **Join Instantly**: 1-tap join, zero swiping or resume-like profiles.
   - **Host Your Own Circle**: Anyone can set time, public venue, and headcount.
4. **Key Features & Personalization**:
   - Set radius (1 km to 20 km).
   - Skill level filtering (Beginner, Intermediate, Pro).
   - Smart Activity Feed (filter by tonight, under 3 km).
   - AI Place Suggestions (safe, public, well-lit cafes and grounds).
5. **Trust & Safety**:
   - Verified student & user profiles (phone + college email + mandatory selfie check before meetup).
   - AI-approved public venues only (CCTV monitored, well-lit).
   - Live location sharing & 1-tap SOS panic button.
   - Women-only circles filter.
   - Zero-tolerance moderation.
6. **Powered by AI**:
   - Multi-signal recommender engine: Granular hobbies, skill ratings, micro-radius, reliability score.
   - Hybrid architecture: Heuristic rules for cold-start campus pilot scaling to collaborative ML embeddings.
7. **Business Model**:
   - 100% Free for users.
   - Verified Venue & Café Partners (sponsored placement & table reservations).
   - Sponsored Brand Circles (gyms, studios, equipment brands).
   - SkillMate Plus (power-user features for organizers).
8. **Roadmap**:
   - Phase 1: BBDNITM Campus Pilot (100 students, 4 weeks, 10+ circles).
   - Phase 2: Lucknow Metro Expansion (Partner cafes, turf grounds, skill streaks).
   - Phase 3: Ecosystem (Virtual audio rooms, smartwatch health tracking).

## 💻 How to View & Run

### Direct In-Browser
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser:
```powershell
Start-Process "C:\Users\nancy\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\skillmate\index.html"
```

### Local HTTP Server
Run the included PowerShell server:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Users\nancy\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\skillmate\serve.ps1"
```
Then navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
